## 2. 10-Slide Investor Pitch Outline

### Slide 1: Title & Vision
- **Header:** CropConnect — Field to Feast, Direct.
- **Sub-header:** Karnataka’s first demand-driven, zero-wastage B2B produce marketplace.
- **Visual:** Side-by-side photo of a Kolar tomato farmer and a Bangalore palace wedding banquet banquet hall with an interconnected green line.

### Slide 2: The Multi-Billion Dollar Broken Pipeline
- **Problem Statement:**
  1. *Predatory Intermediation:* Farmers keep only ₹12–15 on a ₹35/kg vegetable retail transaction.
  2. *Buyer Chaos:* A caterer preparing food for 3,000 guests cannot predict if capsicum will cost ₹40/kg or ₹85/kg on event morning.
  3. *Food Graveyard:* Over 40% of fresh fruits and vegetables rot at collection centers or APMC mandi floors.

### Slide 3: The CropConnect Disruption
- **Solution:** A digital forward-order exchange that turns speculative farming into demand-guaranteed harvesting.
- **Core Pillars:**
  - Forward contracts (24–48h lock-in).
  - WhatsApp conversational UI (Audio/Kannada/English).
  - Farm-gate QC with digital grade certification (A/B grading).

### Slide 4: Target Market & TAM (Karnataka Focus First)
- **TAM (India B2B Fresh Produce):** $65 Billion+.
- **SAM (Karnataka Bulk Hospitality & Institutional Buyers):** $4.2 Billion.
  - Bengaluru hosts 100,000+ major weddings and banquets annually.
  - Average wedding consumes 1.5 to 3.5 tonnes of fresh vegetables and fruits.
- **SOM (Year 1–2 Target):** $25 Million GMV across Bengaluru, Mysuru, and Kolar corridor.

### Slide 5: The Product Experience
- **Buyer Workflow (Caterer):**
  - 06:00 AM: Receives categorized wholesale rate sheet on WhatsApp.
  - 06:05 AM: Types quantities & banquet delivery date/time.
  - 06:06 AM: Order confirmed with guaranteed price lock & tracking ID.
- **Farmer Workflow (FPO/Grower):**
  - Receives harvest batch token via voice note/WhatsApp in Kannada.
  - Drops harvest at local aggregation point (no travel to distant mandis).
  - Receives T+0 direct bank payment upon weight verification.

### Slide 6: Unit Economics & Monetization Model
- **Average Order Value (AOV):** ₹18,500 – ₹45,000 per catering event.
- **Revenue Breakdown:**
  - 4% Platform Fee: ₹1,200 per order average.
  - Logistics Pooling Optimization: ₹650 per order.
  - Gross Margin per Order: ~10% (~₹1,850).
- **Buyer Savings:** Saves 18–25% compared to ad-hoc mandi buying.
- **Farmer Realization:** Earns 35–45% higher than local broker gate prices.

### Slide 7: Operations & Asset-Light Logistics
- **Zero Warehousing Strategy:** We do not build expensive multi-crore cold storages. Produce moves directly from farm-gate aggregation points to event kitchens within 12–14 hours of clipping.
- **Third-Party Logistics (3PL) Pooling:** Partnering with regional mini-truck operators (Tata Ace / Ashok Leyland Dost fleets) on milk-run routes.

### Slide 8: Go-To-Market (GTM) Playbook
1. **Supply Side (Phase 1):** Anchor 15 leading FPOs in Kolar (Tomatoes/Chilli) and Mandya (Greens/Bananas).
2. **Demand Side (Phase 1):** Direct onboarding of Top 100 Bengaluru wedding caterers & banquet hall associations (Palace Grounds, Sheesh Mahal, Whitefield convention centers).
3. **Virality & Referrals:** Word-of-mouth referral bonus for head chefs and event managers.

### Slide 9: Competitive Differentiation
| Feature | Traditional Mandi / APMC | General B2C Quick-Commerce (Blinkit/Zepto) | CropConnect B2B |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Middleman Margin** | 30–50% (High) | 40–60% markups | **3–5% flat** |
| **Order Volume** | Bulk (Opaque) | Retail (1–2 kg) | **Bulk (100kg – 5,000kg)** |
| **Lead Time** | Real-time spot | 10 minutes | **24–48h Advance Lock** |
| **Wastage Rate** | 35–40% | 8–12% (Dark stores) | **< 2% (Pre-harvest lock)** |
| **Interface** | Physical / Cash | Mobile App | **WhatsApp + Web** |
