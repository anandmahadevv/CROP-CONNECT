The Industry Crisis

The Traditional Mandi System is Costing Everyone.

Between the farm gate and the hotel kitchen, 4 to 6 non-value-adding middlemen inflate costs, delay delivery, and allow fresh produce to spoil.

📉
Pain Point 01

Unfair Pricing for Farmers

Growers sacrifice 30% to 50% of their earned crop margin to unregulated commission agents, mandi weighing fees, and loading kickbacks. They bear 100% of the climate risk for the lowest yield share.

Result: Farmers forced into chronic debt cycles.
⚡
Pain Point 02

Quality & Supply Volatility

Wedding caterers and hotel chefs endure erratic price surges and uninspected quality mixing. A sudden 40% tomato price spike on the day of a 2,000-guest reception crushes caterer bottom lines.

Result: Catering margins vanish on event day.
🗑️
Pain Point 03

Severe Perishable Produce Wastage

Without advance pre-order matching, farmers harvest speculatively. Unsold crates sit in hot mandi yards for days, causing up to 40% rotting and post-harvest dump losses.

Result: Millions of tons of nutritious food wasted.
